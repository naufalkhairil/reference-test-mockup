## Question 1

What's an effective strategy for quickly validating a new AI tool in a business setting?

A. Conduct broad, comprehensive market research before implementation

B. Choose a small, representative group of users to run a pilot

C. Jump straight into a large-scale rollout

D. Wait for a competitor to test the approach first, and then follow their lead

<details>
<summary>Show answer and rationale</summary>

**Answer: B — Choose a small, representative group of users to run a pilot**

A small-scale pilot effectively controls risk, avoiding project failure caused by immature technology or a poor fit with the business scenario.
