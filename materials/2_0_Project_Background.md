## Text version

In this chapter, you will start the first project of this course.
You work at an educational content development company. As new employees keep joining, frequent Q&A needs create significant time and resource costs.



To address this, the company compiled a comprehensive document library covering company policies, writing standards, day-to-day tool guides, and more. New employees can now look things up on their own to find the information they need.



After a while, the company found that new employees' work still didn't comply with the policies and errors were frequent.
Analysis showed the cause: the document library held too much information for new employees to read and absorb thoroughly.



To tackle this further, the company built a Q&A system, pre-configured with employees' high-frequency questions so that employees could ask questions themselves.



After the Q&A system had run for a while, people found that the volume of employee inquiries and work errors still hadn't dropped significantly.
Company leaders realized the current Q&A system had many limitations — chiefly, because it relied on fixed rules and similarity matching, it often couldn't answer when employees phrased questions differently.
Moreover, manually configuring question-answer pairs was labor-intensive, with a very low return on investment.



Given this situation, company leaders decided to put you in charge, hoping you could solve the problem once and for all.
After some research, you plan to use today's most advanced LLM technology to build an intelligent Q&A application. An LLM-based intelligent application understands human language well and, without configuring question-answer pairs, can accurately answer questions from the company's internal knowledge base — truly and effectively reducing employee inquiries and improving work efficiency.



Next, you'll roll up your sleeves and make it happen.
