# 5.1 Cultivating "Taste": Using AI to Boost Business Efficiency

## 🚄 Preface

You've learned the technologies from RAG to Agents, and you're surely thinking about how to apply AI to your own business. From customer-service Q&A, contract review, content translation, and coding assistance, to data analysis — every direction seems valuable, and every business need feels urgent. Where do you start so you can both land quickly and meet business needs, generating greater business value? What you need is not just the ability to build systems, but the taste to choose directions, set standards, and validate results.

## 🍁 Course Objectives

**You will learn:**
- The four stages of applying AI in business — the RIDE methodology
- How to judge whether a business scenario is suitable for AI, and how to drive AI adoption in the business
- How to consciously cultivate your own "taste" and level up your AI skills

---

This lesson covers the complete RIDE methodology, helping you build systematic thinking for "choosing directions, setting standards, driving adoption, and validating results." This content is essential for understanding the whole course's design philosophy, preparing for the ACP certification exam, and driving AI to boost business efficiency in real work.
