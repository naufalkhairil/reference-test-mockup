# 5.2 Summary and Outlook

Through the lessons before this one, you started from a single line of API calling and, step by step, built out a complete AI system:

- In the first stage, you got the bot talking, and worked through the first cost calculation
- In the second stage, you gave it knowledge, taught it conventions, and built out evaluation, making answer quality measurable
- In the third stage, you gave it the ability to act, to plan, and to divide labor, gave it memory and methodology, and used evaluation and Harness Engineering to make it keep getting better with use
- In the fourth stage, you solved the cost, deployment, and security problems, taking the system into production
- In the fifth stage, you used the RIDE methodology to look back over the whole project, building the judgment needed to evaluate an AI application's value from a business perspective

Looking back, none of these steps were designed out of thin air — **each one emerged naturally because finishing the previous step surfaced a new problem**.

This isn't just the structure of a single course — it's also the mindset you'll want to bring the next time you build an AI system on your own: first make it work, then make it good, then make it reliable, and finally, take it live.

### 3.1 Further Reading: The Deep-Thinking Model QwQ
The QwQ large model is a strong-reasoning AI model trained on the Qwen2.5 series, available in both a commercial and an open-source version. The commercial version (like qwq-plus) significantly boosts performance on math, code, and general-purpose tasks through reinforcement learning, with core metrics rivaling the full-size version of DeepSeek-R1, and offers both a stable release and the latest snapshot version to choose from; the open-source version (like qwq-32b) also performs excellently, surpassing comparable distilled models. The model supports an extremely long context (131,072 tokens) and multi-turn conversation, enabling ongoing dialogue scenarios, but doesn't support features like tool calling or structured output. The model's design prioritizes reasoning quality, making it well suited to application scenarios that need deep thinking and high-quality responses.

You can [try out the QwQ series of models](https://bailian.console.aliyun.com/#/efm/model_experience_center/text?currentTab=textChat&modelId=qwq-plus-latest) on Bailian, and check the Alibaba Cloud Help Center for [more information](https://help.aliyun.com/zh/model-studio/user-guide/qwq).
